Your Child's Journey to Excellence Starts Here

Welcome to the Albesta Academy admissions entry gateway. We seek to admit students who demonstrate an active willingness to learn, grow, and build strong moral character within our rigorous academic framework.

Our admissions cycle remains open, deliberate, and supportive at every stage. From filling out your initial structural parameters online to sit-down orientation screenings, our administrative registry block handles your child's tracking profile with dedicated care.

General Document Checklist

  • Copy of student's certified Birth Certificate
  • Recent academic transcripts or report sheets from previous schools
  • Two passport photographs (white background)
  • Completed, printed Admission Application Form
The Onboarding Flow

Four Simple Entry Milestones

We have simplified our screening and enrollment framework down to 4 critical, transparent phases.

01

Obtain & Complete Forms

Purchase the official application booklet directly at the school's administrative front desk or click our digital portal system below to input details instantly.

02

Submit Documentation

Return all completed profile folders alongside the required passport prints, transcript verification sets, and the entry processing fee receipts.

03

Entrance Assessment

Prospective applicants will sit for standard written evaluations covering quantitative reasoning, logic patterns, and basic literacy skills.

04

Liaison Interview & Payment

Successful candidates and their guardians are invited for a quick interactive session before structural tuition bills are generated to finalize placement.
