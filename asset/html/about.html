ABOUT DELIGHT ACADEMY

Knowledge is the Ark of Life

A Community Grounded in Faith & Excellence

Delight Academy is a government-approved, co-educational day and boarding primary and secondary school built to international benchmarks. Nestled within a serene, nine-acre environment along Eleko Beach Road in Lekki, Lagos, our purpose-built campus provides a peaceful retreat from city distractions, optimizing student focus and overall academic output.

Established in 2006, our academy answered a vital structural call to deliver holistic, all-encompassing education. By blending highly rigorous academic training with explicit moral and social development, we actively repair the character gaps often left unaddressed in modern education environments.

We deploy a broad-based, fully integrated curriculum that unifies the strengths of both the Nigerian and British educational frameworks. This deep learning system equips our graduates to step confidently onto both local and international competitive landscapes.

Our Vision

To nurture an enduring legacy of moral, social, and academic excellence in line with values that thoroughly develop students into great achievers capable of building a prosperous nation.

Our Mission

To inculcate the spirit of hard work, honesty, integrity, and courage into students through efficient and effective school organization. We seek to prepare them physically, intellectually, and spiritually for the challenges of tomorrow.

Our Structural Journey

2006 — The Core Founding

Delight Academy was formally launched by Delight Educational Services Limited, under the strategic direction of seasoned educationists looking to elevate the region's academic baseline.

2007 — Official Recognition

The institution secured its formal provisional approval from the Lagos State Ministry of Education, clearing the legislative path to scale operations as a fully verified, compliant school.

2016 — Outstanding Quality Designation

Following a rigorous inspection by the Lagos State Quality Assurance team, the Academy was officially rated an "Outstanding School" across administrative, environmental, and learning metrics.

Our Educational Scope

Nursery & Primary

Focuses on cognitive, motor, and foundational analytical reasoning. We establish an enriched, highly secure learning environment for young minds to build core confidence.

Junior Secondary

Spans the initial three years of secondary study, deep-diving into critical sciences and humanities to prepare students fully for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

Senior Secondary

A highly competitive, specialized track integrating domestic and international curricula, systematically positioning students to clear WASSCE, NECO, IGCSE, and global SAT exams.

Delight Academy

We are deeply committed to fostering a culture of hard work, where students are systematically encouraged to study diligently and achieve exceptional results through effort, character, and perseverance.
