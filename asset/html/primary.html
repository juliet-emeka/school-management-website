Primary School Academic Core

We implement a robust educational framework structured to expand our students' knowledge landscape and completely prepare them for advanced secondary entry challenges.

01

Quantitative & Mathematical Logic

Moving beyond rote calculations to real-world word problems, structural geometry, mathematical analysis models, and data handling fundamentals.

02

Scientific Inquiry & Tech Basics

Encouraging deep hands-on discovery across physical, chemical, and natural sciences alongside regular laboratory observations and introductory coding logics.

03

Linguistics & Literary Analysis

Strengthening complex spelling systems, grammar mechanics, formal creative essay assembly, public presentation, and analytical book review circles.
